Papers

Sign-hedged Beam QAOA: Scaling Quantum Optimization Algorithms

Arush Sharma, Anupama Ray.

Best Paper Award, IBM Research Internship Symposium 2026. Manuscript in preparation. [award]

Combines a power-law schedule found by an evolutionary search over QAOA schedules with width-B sign hedging, so recursive QAOA (RQAOA) no longer makes irreversible sign errors in its correlators. Approximation ratio 0.998 in simulation and 1.000 on IBM Marrakesh hardware for the Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model (n=20); 0.984 on MaxCut (n=60), 17.6% above QAOA.

From Points to Distributions: Probabilistic Latent Autoregression for Medical Image Representation Learning

Arush Sharma, Soumen Basu, Manoj Kumar.

Under review at WACV 2027. [pdf]

PACL predicts a Gaussian latent for each image patch instead of a single point embedding, and carries that uncertainty into classification and segmentation. Chest X-ray AUROC 0.987, breast-ultrasound (BUSI) AUROC 0.938, segmentation IoU 0.604, each above the prior best by 2, 2.1 and 1.3 points. My part: the mean/variance and anti-collapse losses.

BrainRMA: ROI-Preserving Multiscale Attention for Brain Network Analysis

Second author, with Soumen Basu and Manoj Kumar.

Under review at AAAI 2027. [pdf]

Keeps one token per brain region instead of pooling regions away, and fuses global attention with reliability-weighted region-to-cluster context. Accuracy 82.35% on ABIDE and 93.75% on OSF, 3.35 and 6.25 points above the prior best. My part: the ablation suite, and co-writing the methodology and experiments.
