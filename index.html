Research

Under review · WACV 2027 First author

From Points to Distributions: Probabilistic Latent Autoregression for Medical Image Representation Learning

Deterministic self-supervised learning collapses ambiguous anatomy into over-confident point embeddings. PACL predicts a Gaussian latent per patch instead and carries that uncertainty into classification and segmentation. I formulated the mean/variance and anti-collapse losses.

  • 0.987 CXR AUROC
  • 0.938 BUSI AUROC
  • 0.604 IoU
  • +2% / +2.1% / +1.3 pts over prior SOTA

With Dr. Soumen Basu (Meta) and Dr. Manoj Kumar (IIT Roorkee)

Under review · AAAI 2027 Second author

BrainRMA: ROI-Preserving Multiscale Attention for Brain Network Analysis

Brain-graph models either miss long-range dependencies or erase region identity through pooling. BrainRMA keeps ROI tokens and fuses global attention with reliability-weighted ROI-to-cluster context. I ran the ablation suite and co-wrote the methodology and experiments.

  • 82.35% ABIDE
  • 93.75% OSF
  • +3.35 / +6.25 pts over SOTA

With Dr. Soumen Basu (Meta) and Dr. Manoj Kumar (IIT Roorkee)

In preparation Best Paper · IBM Research

Sign-hedged Beam QAOA: Scaling Quantum Optimization Algorithms

Pairs a CliffSearch-evolved power-law schedule with width-B sign hedging, so recursive QAOA no longer makes irreversible correlator-sign errors.

  • 0.998 SK ±1 (n=20), 2.1× AR
  • 1.000 on IBM Marrakesh
  • 0.984 MaxCut n=60, +17.6% vs QAOA
  • 0.983 20-asset portfolio, +26% vs RQAOA

Structure-aware KV-cache eviction for long agent traces
